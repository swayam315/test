The Current Problem

SIH Problem Statement ID 25106 highlights critical inefficiencies

Scattered Communication

Opportunities scattered across WhatsApp groups, emails, and notice boards creating confusion and missed deadlines.

Manual Processes

Heavy reliance on spreadsheets, physical documentation, and endless office visits for approvals.

No Analytics

Lack of real-time insights into placement statistics, trends, and individual student progress.

Inequality

Students from less connected backgrounds suffer more when processes are manual and fragmented.

Impact on Stakeholders

Students

  • Missed deadlines and opportunities
  • Uncertainty about application status
  • No systematic skill documentation

Placement Cells

  • Excessive administrative burden
  • Limited time for career guidance
  • Poor data visibility

Faculty

  • Manual verification processes
  • Complex scheduling coordination
  • Limited student visibility
